## Ad Business Model Framework

Gokul identifies three ways ad businesses make money:

1. **Owned User Audience + Interaction Surface**
   - Example: Google Search (high-intent users expressing intent directly)
   - Example: Facebook (identity data combined with user engagement)
   - ChatGPT has both intent AND identity data (unlike Google which had intent, or Facebook which had identity)

2. **Deliver Outcome at Low Cost**
   - Example: AppLovin ($100B+ company specializing in mobile app installs)
   - Success metric: cost-effectively driving a single outcome

3. **Exclusive Provider Position**
   - Example: The Trade Desk (exclusive demand source for large advertisers)
   - Leverage through supply/demand asymmetry

## Key Insight

ChatGPT's combination of high-intent user data + identity information may make it more powerful for targeted advertising than Google or Facebook, as it captures both dimensions simultaneously.
